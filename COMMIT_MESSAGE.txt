Show a hint and a key to open the Summoning Altar recipes

- When the player looks at a Summoning Rituals indestructible altar, a
  subtle semi-transparent box above the health bar says "Press U to see all
  Summoning Altar recipes" (fades in and out).
- Pressing the key (U by default, configurable in Controls under
  "Beyond The End") opens JEI with every recipe that uses the Altar as
  catalyst.
- Small JEI plugin (JeiAltarRecipes) keeps the JEI runtime; JEI API added as
  compileOnly from maven.blamejared.com (jei_version in gradle.properties).
  Without JEI or Summoning Rituals nothing is shown.
- Lang entries (en_us, es_es).
